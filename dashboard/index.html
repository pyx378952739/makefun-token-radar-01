How the radar prices a launch

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of live protocols in the same category, shrunk toward the global median (4-protocol prior) and clamped to 0.05–1.50. No name is ever special-cased; the snapshot’s own market sets the price.
  • Growth — 1 + clamp(7d change, −50%, +150%) × 0.6. A protocol growing fast is worth more; a shrinking one less.
  • Maturity — 0.7 + min(age, 1095 days) / 1095 × 0.5. A protocol that has run for years with no token has more pent-up launch value. Unknown age counts as 365 days.
  • Breadth — 1 + 0.03 × min(chains, 5). Multi-chain reach is worth a little more.

What this radar does not do

  • It does not predict whether or when a protocol issues a token. A high implied value means the launch would be valuable if the protocol’s record stayed as it is.
  • It takes the snapshot’s record at face value. A protocol on the watchlist may still have a token its record does not carry; a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades is not a new launch.
  • Farm plans are generic by category, chain, size and age. They name no private deal, no insider round and no guaranteed allocation — because the snapshot carries none.
